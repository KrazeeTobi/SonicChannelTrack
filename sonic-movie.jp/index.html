During our 100-year history, Paramount Pictures Studios has served as the production site for thousands of notable movies and TV shows. Our studio lot has grown considerably over the years, expanding to 65 acres and thirty stages, including our one-of-a-kind New York Stret. backlot When you visit our lot, not only will you see where some of the most celebrated movies and television series were made, but also see how we continue to produce iconic motion pictures for the next century.
